ANIMALS MAP PLACES

GIRAFFE

Giraffes are the tallest mammals in the world. Their long necks allow them to reach leaves high in trees that other animals cannot access.

Despite their height, giraffes are graceful animals and can run at impressive speeds across the African savannah.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM?

You will find the giraffes at pen G15 near the viewing deck.
