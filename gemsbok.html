GEMSBOK

The gemsbok is a large antelope native to Southern Africa. It is famous for its long, straight horns and striking black-and-white facial markings.

Gemsbok are perfectly adapted to desert conditions and can survive with very little water by getting moisture from the plants they eat.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM?

You will find the gemsbok at pen G08 near the desert habitat.
